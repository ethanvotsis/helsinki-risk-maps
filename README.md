# helsinki-risk-maps
This web map is part of a climate risks project commissioned by Helsinki municipality to the Finnish Meteorological Institute. 

The map contains several layers that give a sense of the geographical distribution of social parameters important for urban climate change adaptation and disaster risk preparedness and management. These are: 

(1) Age groups known to be vulnerable to climate impacts (e.g. heatwaves).
(2) Health-related infrastructure (hospitals, health centers, and elderly care gomes).
(3) House price hot and cold spots.  

The map was used during the project to select, develop and fine-tune the geovisualisations that were included in the final report, accessible at https://www.hel.fi/static/liitteet/kaupunkiymparisto/julkaisut/julkaisut/julkaisu-32-19-en.pdf.
